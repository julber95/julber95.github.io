I study artificial intelligence at Université PSL, in the IASD master's programme, and work as an AI engineer
on a work-study contract. Before that I did a double bachelor's in AI and economics at Paris-Dauphine.

What draws me to machine learning is usually the part *after* the training loop: how a GAN's discriminator can
filter what the generator makes, how decoding changes what a language model says, and whether an explanation
survives when you delete the words it points to. I tend to prefer small models that can be read.

On the side, I've done visual-identity and graphic-design work as a volunteer, for ALIAS (2024–2025) and
earlier through France Bénévolat (2020). This site is drawn in code for the same reason.
